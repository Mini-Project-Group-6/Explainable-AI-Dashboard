SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Comprehension Skills
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 30

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- evaluate a curriculum indicator for Comprehension Skills using at least three evidence
- identify the key idea of passage in one lesson
- explain main idea with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for passage, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
specimens of passage; a projector where power is available; counters for the group task.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher reviews learners' previous knowledge of inference. This is connected to today's topic, Comprehension Skills, and in particular to passage. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on passage in pairs.
(10 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about main idea.
(10 minutes) In groups, learners investigate summary and record their observations.
(20 minutes) Learners discuss context clues in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(15 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of inference in their exercise books.
(20 minutes) Teacher reads the section on passage from the textbook.

EVALUATION:
1. Describe how passage occurs.
2. Calculate the answer to the exercise on main idea on the board.
3. Calculate the answer to the exercise on summary on the board.
4. Describe how context clues occurs.
5. Calculate the answer to the exercise on inference on the board.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Groups are mixed ability so learners support one another. A challenge question is provided for the most able.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain passage.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt.
