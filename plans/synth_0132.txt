SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Area of Plane Shapes
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 40

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- solve the main idea of length
- solve formula

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for length, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
a whiteboard marker for key terms; specimens of formula.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Learners recall what they already know about rectangle. This is connected to today's topic, Area of Plane Shapes, and in particular to length. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Area of Plane Shapes involves length. Learners should note that length is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves formula. Learners should note that formula is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves square. Learners should note that square is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves triangle. Learners should note that triangle is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves breadth. Learners should note that breadth is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves rectangle. Learners should note that rectangle is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves length. Learners should note that length is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves formula. Learners should note that formula is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on length in pairs.
(15 minutes) In groups, learners investigate formula and record their observations.
(20 minutes) Teacher explains square to the class.
(10 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about triangle.
(15 minutes) Teacher demonstrates breadth using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.

EVALUATION:
1. Describe how length occurs.
2. Explain the role of formula in your own words.
3. Describe how square occurs.
4. Explain the role of triangle in your own words.
5. Calculate the answer to the exercise on breadth on the board.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys. Learners may first discuss in their mother tongue. A challenge question is provided for the most able. Learners are grouped by ability for the practice task.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain length clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners state the key points to the class.
