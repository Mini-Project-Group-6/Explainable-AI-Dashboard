SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: The Water Cycle
CLASS: JHS 1
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 28

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- compare the main idea of clouds
- list precipitation

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for clouds.

RESOURCES:
a whiteboard marker for key terms; a ruler and measuring tape.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Learners recall what they already know about rainfall. This is connected to today's topic, The Water Cycle, and in particular to clouds. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
The Water Cycle involves clouds. Learners should note that clouds is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves precipitation. Learners should note that precipitation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves rivers. Learners should note that rivers is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves condensation. Learners should note that condensation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves evaporation. Learners should note that evaporation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves rainfall. Learners should note that rainfall is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(10 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on clouds in pairs.
(15 minutes) Teacher explains precipitation to the class.
(20 minutes) Learners discuss rivers in small groups and present their findings to the class.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by clouds?
2. Explain the role of precipitation in your own words.
3. Explain the role of rivers in your own words.
4. What is meant by condensation?

DIFFERENTIATION:
Questions are directed equally across the class.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain clouds.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
