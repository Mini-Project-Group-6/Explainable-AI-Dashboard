SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: Simple Machines
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 39

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- compare a curriculum indicator for Simple Machines using at least two evidence
- list the key idea of inclined plane in one lesson
- explain pulley with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for inclined plane, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
worksheets on inclined plane; cardboard cut-outs prepared beforehand; a phone image of load.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher reviews learners' previous knowledge of effort. This is connected to today's topic, Simple Machines, and in particular to inclined plane. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Simple Machines involves inclined plane. Learners should note that inclined plane is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves pulley. Learners should note that pulley is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves load. Learners should note that load is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves lever. Learners should note that lever is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves fulcrum. Learners should note that fulcrum is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Teacher reads the section on inclined plane from the textbook.
(20 minutes) Learners discuss pulley in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(15 minutes) Teacher demonstrates load using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(15 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of lever in their exercise books.
(15 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about fulcrum.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by inclined plane?
2. Explain the role of pulley in your own words.
3. Explain the role of load in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Key terms are explained in the local language where needed. Provision is made for a learner with a hearing impairment. Learners are grouped by ability for the practice task.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain inclined plane.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
