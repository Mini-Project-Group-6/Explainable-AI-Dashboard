SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Area of Plane Shapes
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 90 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 44

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- identify the main idea of triangle
- describe formula

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for triangle, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
chalkboard and chalk; flashcards for formula; real objects collected from the school compound; a short video clip on rectangle.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher revises the previous lesson on square. This is connected to today's topic, Area of Plane Shapes, and in particular to triangle.

CORE POINTS:
Area of Plane Shapes involves triangle. Learners should note that triangle is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
Learners discuss triangle in small groups and present their findings to the class.
Teacher explains formula to the class.
In groups, learners investigate breadth and record their observations.
Teacher explains rectangle to the class.

EVALUATION:
1. Describe how triangle occurs.
2. Calculate the answer to the exercise on formula on the board.
3. What is meant by breadth?

DIFFERENTIATION:
An extension task is ready for learners who finish early. Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys. Provision is made for a learner with a hearing impairment. Groups are mixed ability so learners support one another.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain triangle clearly.

SEQUENCING:
A phase is missing and timings are absent.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt.
