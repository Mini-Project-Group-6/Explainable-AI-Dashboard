SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: Simple Machines
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 47

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- appreciate load

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for load.

RESOURCES:
cardboard cut-outs prepared beforehand; specimens of fulcrum; a whiteboard marker for key terms.

INTRODUCTION:
Learners recall what they already know about inclined plane. This is connected to today's topic, Simple Machines, and in particular to load.

CORE POINTS:
Simple Machines involves load. Learners should note that load is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves fulcrum. Learners should note that fulcrum is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves lever. Learners should note that lever is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves pulley. Learners should note that pulley is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves effort. Learners should note that effort is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves inclined plane. Learners should note that inclined plane is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Learners discuss load in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(20 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on fulcrum in pairs.
(15 minutes) Teacher explains lever to the class.
(10 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on pulley in pairs.
(10 minutes) In groups, learners investigate effort and record their observations.

EVALUATION:
1. List 4 examples of load.
2. What is meant by fulcrum?
3. Describe how lever occurs.
4. Explain the role of pulley in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Groups are mixed ability so learners support one another. A challenge question is provided for the most able.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain load clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
