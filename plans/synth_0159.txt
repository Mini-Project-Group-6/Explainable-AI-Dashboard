SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: States of Matter
CLASS: JHS 1
DURATION: 105 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 39

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- calculate a curriculum indicator for States of Matter using with the aid of real objects evidence
- describe the key idea of liquid in one lesson
- identify boiling with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for liquid.

RESOURCES:
chalkboard and chalk.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher revises the previous lesson on particles. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
States of Matter involves liquid. Learners should note that liquid is a key point of today's lesson.
States of Matter involves boiling. Learners should note that boiling is a key point of today's lesson.
States of Matter involves melting. Learners should note that melting is a key point of today's lesson.
States of Matter involves solid. Learners should note that solid is a key point of today's lesson.
States of Matter involves gas. Learners should note that gas is a key point of today's lesson.
States of Matter involves particles. Learners should note that particles is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
In groups, learners investigate liquid and record their observations.
Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about boiling.
Teacher writes notes about melting on the board for learners to copy.
Learners role-play the process of solid in front of the class.
Learners discuss gas in small groups and present their findings to the class.

EVALUATION:
1. Explain the role of liquid in your own words.
2. Calculate the answer to the exercise on boiling on the board.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Learners are grouped by ability for the practice task. A challenge question is provided for the most able.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain liquid clearly.

SEQUENCING:
A phase is missing and timings are absent.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners state the key points to the class.
