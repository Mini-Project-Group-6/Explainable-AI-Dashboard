SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Area of Plane Shapes
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 36

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- state a curriculum indicator for Area of Plane Shapes using correctly evidence
- demonstrate the key idea of rectangle in one lesson
- identify square with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for rectangle, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
cardboard cut-outs prepared beforehand; a labelled diagram of square.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Learners recall what they already know about triangle. This is connected to today's topic, Area of Plane Shapes, and in particular to rectangle. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Area of Plane Shapes involves rectangle. Learners should note that rectangle is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves square. Learners should note that square is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves formula. Learners should note that formula is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves breadth. Learners should note that breadth is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves length. Learners should note that length is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves triangle. Learners should note that triangle is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves rectangle. Learners should note that rectangle is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves square. Learners should note that square is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(10 minutes) Learners role-play the process of rectangle in front of the class.
(15 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of square in their exercise books.
(15 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on formula in pairs.
(15 minutes) Teacher demonstrates breadth using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(10 minutes) Teacher writes notes about length on the board for learners to copy.
(10 minutes) Teacher demonstrates triangle using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.

EVALUATION:
1. Calculate the answer to the exercise on rectangle on the board.
2. Describe how square occurs.
3. Describe how formula occurs.
4. Explain the role of breadth in your own words.
5. Describe how length occurs.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Slower learners receive targeted support during the group work. Questions are directed equally across the class. Learners are grouped by ability for the practice task.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain rectangle.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
