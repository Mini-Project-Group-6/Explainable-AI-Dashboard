SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Nouns and Pronouns
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 42

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- explain a curriculum indicator for Nouns and Pronouns using at least three evidence
- describe the key idea of proper noun in one lesson
- state pronoun with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for proper noun, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
a simple model of proper noun; a whiteboard marker for key terms.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher reviews learners' previous knowledge of common noun. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Nouns and Pronouns involves proper noun. Learners should note that proper noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves pronoun. Learners should note that pronoun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves noun. Learners should note that noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves subject. Learners should note that subject is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves sentence. Learners should note that sentence is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves common noun. Learners should note that common noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves proper noun. Learners should note that proper noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves pronoun. Learners should note that pronoun is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of proper noun in their exercise books.
(15 minutes) Teacher demonstrates pronoun using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(20 minutes) Teacher reads the section on noun from the textbook.
(20 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on subject in pairs.
(20 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on sentence in pairs.
(10 minutes) Teacher demonstrates common noun using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by proper noun?
2. Calculate the answer to the exercise on pronoun on the board.
3. Explain the role of noun in your own words.
4. Describe how subject occurs.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Key terms are explained in the local language where needed. Learners are grouped by ability for the practice task. Questions are directed equally across the class.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain proper noun.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners state the key points to the class. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
