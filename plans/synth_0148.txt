SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: The Water Cycle
CLASS: JHS 1
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 46

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- calculate a curriculum indicator for The Water Cycle using at least two evidence
- calculate the key idea of precipitation in one lesson
- demonstrate condensation with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for precipitation.

RESOURCES:
real objects collected from the school compound; a short video clip on condensation; flashcards for rivers; counters for the group task.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Learners recall what they already know about rainfall. This is connected to today's topic, The Water Cycle, and in particular to precipitation. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
The Water Cycle involves precipitation. Learners should note that precipitation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves condensation. Learners should note that condensation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves rivers. Learners should note that rivers is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves evaporation. Learners should note that evaporation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves clouds. Learners should note that clouds is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves rainfall. Learners should note that rainfall is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Teacher writes notes about precipitation on the board for learners to copy.
(10 minutes) Teacher demonstrates condensation using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(10 minutes) Teacher demonstrates rivers using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(10 minutes) In groups, learners investigate evaporation and record their observations.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by precipitation?
2. Describe how condensation occurs.
3. Calculate the answer to the exercise on rivers on the board.
4. List 3 examples of evaporation.
5. Calculate the answer to the exercise on clouds on the board.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Provision is made for a learner with a hearing impairment.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain precipitation clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
