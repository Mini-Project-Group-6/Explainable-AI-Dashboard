SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Comprehension Skills
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 49

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- explain a curriculum indicator for Comprehension Skills using using the chart provided evidence
- classify the key idea of main idea in one lesson
- solve inference with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for main idea, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
a phone image of main idea; a whiteboard marker for key terms; the class textbook; cardboard cut-outs prepared beforehand.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher reviews learners' previous knowledge of summary. This is connected to today's topic, Comprehension Skills, and in particular to main idea. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(10 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of main idea in their exercise books.
(10 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of inference in their exercise books.
(10 minutes) Learners role-play the process of passage in front of the class.
(20 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about context clues.
(10 minutes) Teacher writes notes about summary on the board for learners to copy.
(10 minutes) Learners role-play the process of main idea in front of the class.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by main idea?
2. Explain the role of inference in your own words.
3. List 3 examples of passage.
4. Describe how context clues occurs.
5. Explain the role of summary in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Provision is made for a learner with a hearing impairment. Learners are grouped by ability for the practice task. Questions are directed equally across the class.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain main idea.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
