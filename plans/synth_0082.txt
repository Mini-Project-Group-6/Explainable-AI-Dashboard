SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: Simple Machines
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 34

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- appreciate effort

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for effort.

RESOURCES:
a short video clip on effort; counters for the group task.

INTRODUCTION:
This is connected to today's topic, Simple Machines, and in particular to effort. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Simple Machines involves effort. Learners should note that effort is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves inclined plane. Learners should note that inclined plane is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves pulley. Learners should note that pulley is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves fulcrum. Learners should note that fulcrum is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves load. Learners should note that load is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves lever. Learners should note that lever is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(10 minutes) In groups, learners investigate effort and record their observations.
(15 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about inclined plane.
(20 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on pulley in pairs.
(10 minutes) Learners discuss fulcrum in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(10 minutes) Teacher reads the section on load from the textbook.

EVALUATION:
1. Explain the role of effort in your own words.
2. Describe how inclined plane occurs.
3. Describe how pulley occurs.
4. Explain the role of fulcrum in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Learners may first discuss in their mother tongue. Slower learners receive targeted support during the group work.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain effort.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt.
