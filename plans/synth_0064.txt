OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- solve the main idea of main idea
- state summary

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for main idea, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
flashcards for main idea; a whiteboard marker for key terms; cardboard cut-outs prepared beforehand; a short video clip on context clues.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Learners recall what they already know about passage. This is connected to today's topic, Comprehension Skills, and in particular to main idea. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Teacher demonstrates main idea using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.

(20 minutes) Learners role-play the process of inference in front of the class.
(20 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on context clues in pairs.

1. Describe how main idea occurs.

DIFFERENTIATION:
An extension task is ready for learners who finish early. Learners may first discuss in their mother tongue. Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain main idea.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
