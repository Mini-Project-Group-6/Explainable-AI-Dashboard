SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: Photosynthesis
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 39

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- identify the main idea of sunlight
- list carbon dioxide

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for sunlight.

RESOURCES:
a wall chart showing sunlight; a ruler and measuring tape; a simple model of leaf; a phone image of chlorophyll.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher reviews learners' previous knowledge of oxygen. This is connected to today's topic, Photosynthesis, and in particular to sunlight. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Photosynthesis involves sunlight. Learners should note that sunlight is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves carbon dioxide. Learners should note that carbon dioxide is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves leaf. Learners should note that leaf is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves chlorophyll. Learners should note that chlorophyll is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves glucose. Learners should note that glucose is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves plants. Learners should note that plants is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Teacher explains sunlight to the class.
(20 minutes) Learners discuss carbon dioxide in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(20 minutes) Learners role-play the process of leaf in front of the class.
(10 minutes) In groups, learners investigate chlorophyll and record their observations.
(20 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on glucose in pairs.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by sunlight?
2. List 3 examples of carbon dioxide.
3. What is meant by leaf?
4. Describe how chlorophyll occurs.
5. What is meant by glucose?

DIFFERENTIATION:
Slower learners receive targeted support during the group work. Learners may first discuss in their mother tongue. Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain sunlight.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners state the key points to the class. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
