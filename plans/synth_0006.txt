SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Comprehension Skills
CLASS: JHS 1
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 40

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- classify the main idea of inference
- calculate passage

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for inference.

RESOURCES:
a projector where power is available; a labelled diagram of passage.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. This is connected to today's topic, Comprehension Skills, and in particular to inference.

CORE POINTS:
Comprehension Skills involves inference. Learners should note that inference is a key point of today's lesson.
Comprehension Skills involves passage. Learners should note that passage is a key point of today's lesson.
Comprehension Skills involves context clues. Learners should note that context clues is a key point of today's lesson.
Comprehension Skills involves main idea. Learners should note that main idea is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Learners role-play the process of inference in front of the class.
(10 minutes) In groups, learners investigate passage and record their observations.
(15 minutes) Teacher reads the section on context clues from the textbook.
(15 minutes) Teacher explains main idea to the class.
(10 minutes) Learners discuss summary in small groups and present their findings to the class.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by inference?
2. What is meant by passage?
3. Explain the role of context clues in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys. A challenge question is provided for the most able. Learners may first discuss in their mother tongue.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain inference clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
