SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: Photosynthesis
CLASS: JHS 1
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 31

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- list the main idea of carbon dioxide
- solve sunlight

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for carbon dioxide.

RESOURCES:
a ruler and measuring tape; chalkboard and chalk.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Learners recall what they already know about chlorophyll. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Photosynthesis involves carbon dioxide. Learners should note that carbon dioxide is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves sunlight. Learners should note that sunlight is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves leaf. Learners should note that leaf is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves glucose. Learners should note that glucose is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about carbon dioxide.
(10 minutes) Learners role-play the process of sunlight in front of the class.
(20 minutes) Teacher writes notes about leaf on the board for learners to copy.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by carbon dioxide?
2. What is meant by sunlight?

DIFFERENTIATION:
Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys. A challenge question is provided for the most able. Learners are grouped by ability for the practice task.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain carbon dioxide clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Learners state the key points to the class. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
