SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Percentages
CLASS: JHS 1
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 44

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- appreciate conversion

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for conversion.

RESOURCES:
chalkboard and chalk.

INTRODUCTION:
Learners recall what they already know about percent.

CORE POINTS:
Percentages involves conversion. Learners should note that conversion is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves fraction. Learners should note that fraction is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves decimal. Learners should note that decimal is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves discount. Learners should note that discount is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves profit. Learners should note that profit is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves percent. Learners should note that percent is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Teacher explains conversion to the class.
(10 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on fraction in pairs.
(15 minutes) Learners role-play the process of decimal in front of the class.
(20 minutes) Teacher reads the section on discount from the textbook.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by conversion?
2. Explain the role of fraction in your own words.
3. Calculate the answer to the exercise on decimal on the board.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Learners with special educational needs are seated in front and given adapted materials.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain conversion clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
