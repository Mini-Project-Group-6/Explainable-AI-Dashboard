SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Fractions
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 36

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- solve the main idea of addition
- evaluate equivalent fractions

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for addition.

RESOURCES:
the class textbook; cardboard cut-outs prepared beforehand.

INTRODUCTION:
The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Fractions involves addition. Learners should note that addition is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves equivalent fractions. Learners should note that equivalent fractions is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves numerator. Learners should note that numerator is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves simplify. Learners should note that simplify is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about addition.
(15 minutes) Teacher writes notes about equivalent fractions on the board for learners to copy.
(20 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of numerator in their exercise books.
(20 minutes) Learners discuss simplify in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(15 minutes) Learners role-play the process of denominator in front of the class.

EVALUATION:
1. Calculate the answer to the exercise on addition on the board.
2. List 4 examples of equivalent fractions.
3. List 3 examples of numerator.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Provision is made for a learner with a hearing impairment.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain addition clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt.
