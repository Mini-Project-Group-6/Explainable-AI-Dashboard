SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Nouns and Pronouns
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 29

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- solve the main idea of common noun
- describe subject

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for common noun.

RESOURCES:
flashcards for common noun; cardboard cut-outs prepared beforehand.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher revises the previous lesson on noun. This is connected to today's topic, Nouns and Pronouns, and in particular to common noun. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Nouns and Pronouns involves common noun. Learners should note that common noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves subject. Learners should note that subject is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves proper noun. Learners should note that proper noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves sentence. Learners should note that sentence is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves pronoun. Learners should note that pronoun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves noun. Learners should note that noun is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Teacher reads the section on common noun from the textbook.
(20 minutes) Teacher demonstrates subject using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(20 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about proper noun.
(15 minutes) Learners role-play the process of sentence in front of the class.

EVALUATION:
1. Copy the notes from the board neatly into your exercise book.
2. Copy the notes from the board neatly into your exercise book.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Learners may first discuss in their mother tongue.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain common noun clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners state the key points to the class. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
