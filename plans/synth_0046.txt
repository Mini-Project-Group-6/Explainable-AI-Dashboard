SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Nouns and Pronouns
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 44

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- demonstrate the main idea of subject
- solve sentence

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for subject.

RESOURCES:
a simple model of subject; counters for the group task; a labelled diagram of pronoun.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher revises the previous lesson on noun.

CORE POINTS:
Nouns and Pronouns involves subject. Learners should note that subject is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves sentence. Learners should note that sentence is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves pronoun. Learners should note that pronoun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves proper noun. Learners should note that proper noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves common noun. Learners should note that common noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves noun. Learners should note that noun is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Learners discuss subject in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(10 minutes) In groups, learners investigate sentence and record their observations.
(10 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on pronoun in pairs.
(20 minutes) Teacher writes notes about proper noun on the board for learners to copy.
(15 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of common noun in their exercise books.

EVALUATION:
1. Calculate the answer to the exercise on subject on the board.
2. Describe how sentence occurs.
3. Explain the role of pronoun in your own words.
4. List 3 examples of proper noun.
5. List 3 examples of common noun.

DIFFERENTIATION:
A support task with scaffolded steps is prepared for learners who struggle. Learners are grouped by ability for the practice task.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain subject.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
