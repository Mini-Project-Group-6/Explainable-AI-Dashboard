SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Comprehension Skills
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 42

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- describe the main idea of summary
- identify inference

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for summary.

RESOURCES:
cardboard cut-outs prepared beforehand; a short video clip on inference.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher revises the previous lesson on context clues. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Comprehension Skills involves summary. Learners should note that summary is a key point of today's lesson.
Comprehension Skills involves inference. Learners should note that inference is a key point of today's lesson.
Comprehension Skills involves main idea. Learners should note that main idea is a key point of today's lesson.
Comprehension Skills involves passage. Learners should note that passage is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) In groups, learners investigate summary and record their observations.
(10 minutes) Teacher explains inference to the class.
(15 minutes) In groups, learners investigate main idea and record their observations.
(15 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of passage in their exercise books.
(10 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about context clues.

EVALUATION:
1. Explain the role of summary in your own words.
2. Calculate the answer to the exercise on inference on the board.
3. List 4 examples of main idea.
4. Describe how passage occurs.
5. Explain the role of context clues in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
No reference to differing learner needs.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain summary clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
