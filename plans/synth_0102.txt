SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Comprehension Skills
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 43

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- solve the main idea of main idea
- explain context clues

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for main idea, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
a labelled diagram of main idea; a short video clip on context clues; real objects collected from the school compound.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher revises the previous lesson on summary. This is connected to today's topic, Comprehension Skills, and in particular to main idea. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Teacher demonstrates main idea using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(20 minutes) Learners discuss context clues in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(20 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about passage.
(20 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about inference.
(10 minutes) Learners role-play the process of summary in front of the class.
(20 minutes) Teacher reads the section on main idea from the textbook.

EVALUATION:
1. Describe how main idea occurs.
2. What is meant by context clues?
3. Explain the role of passage in your own words.
4. Explain the role of inference in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Groups are mixed ability so learners support one another. Learners may first discuss in their mother tongue. A challenge question is provided for the most able. Provision is made for a learner with a hearing impairment.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain main idea.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
