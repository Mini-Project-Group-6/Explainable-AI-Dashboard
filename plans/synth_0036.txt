SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Nouns and Pronouns
CLASS: JHS 1
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 27

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- compare the main idea of noun
- solve common noun

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for noun.

RESOURCES:
a short video clip on noun; real objects collected from the school compound.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Learners recall what they already know about pronoun. This is connected to today's topic, Nouns and Pronouns, and in particular to noun. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Nouns and Pronouns involves noun. Learners should note that noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves common noun. Learners should note that common noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves sentence. Learners should note that sentence is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves proper noun. Learners should note that proper noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves subject. Learners should note that subject is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves pronoun. Learners should note that pronoun is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) In groups, learners investigate noun and record their observations.
(15 minutes) In groups, learners investigate common noun and record their observations.
(10 minutes) Teacher reads the section on sentence from the textbook.
(10 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of proper noun in their exercise books.
(10 minutes) Learners discuss subject in small groups and present their findings to the class.

EVALUATION:
1. Calculate the answer to the exercise on noun on the board.
2. What is meant by common noun?
3. Calculate the answer to the exercise on sentence on the board.
4. Explain the role of proper noun in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Provision is made for a learner with a hearing impairment. Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain noun clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board.
