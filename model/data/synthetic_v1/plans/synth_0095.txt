SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: Photosynthesis
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 37

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- demonstrate a curriculum indicator for Photosynthesis using using the chart provided evidence
- state the key idea of sunlight in one lesson
- calculate glucose with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for sunlight, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
the class textbook; cardboard cut-outs prepared beforehand; a projector where power is available; specimens of oxygen.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher reviews learners' previous knowledge of plants. This is connected to today's topic, Photosynthesis, and in particular to sunlight. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Photosynthesis involves sunlight. Learners should note that sunlight is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves glucose. Learners should note that glucose is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves leaf. Learners should note that leaf is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves oxygen. Learners should note that oxygen is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves carbon dioxide. Learners should note that carbon dioxide is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves chlorophyll. Learners should note that chlorophyll is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves plants. Learners should note that plants is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves sunlight. Learners should note that sunlight is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Learners discuss sunlight in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(10 minutes) Learners role-play the process of glucose in front of the class.
(10 minutes) Learners discuss leaf in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(10 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about oxygen.
(15 minutes) Teacher writes notes about carbon dioxide on the board for learners to copy.

EVALUATION:
1. Explain the role of sunlight in your own words.
2. What is meant by glucose?
3. Explain the role of leaf in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Learners with special educational needs are seated in front and given adapted materials. Questions are directed equally across the class. Learners are grouped by ability for the practice task.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain sunlight clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
