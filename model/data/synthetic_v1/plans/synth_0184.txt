SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Percentages
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 42

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- construct a curriculum indicator for Percentages using with the aid of real objects evidence
- construct the key idea of percent in one lesson
- calculate discount with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for percent, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
chalkboard and chalk; a phone image of discount; worksheets on conversion.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher revises the previous lesson on fraction. This is connected to today's topic, Percentages, and in particular to percent. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Percentages involves percent. Learners should note that percent is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves discount. Learners should note that discount is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves conversion. Learners should note that conversion is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves profit. Learners should note that profit is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves decimal. Learners should note that decimal is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about percent.
(15 minutes) Teacher demonstrates discount using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(20 minutes) Learners role-play the process of conversion in front of the class.
(15 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about profit.
(10 minutes) Teacher reads the section on decimal from the textbook.

EVALUATION:
1. Explain the role of percent in your own words.
2. List 2 examples of discount.
3. List 3 examples of conversion.
4. What is meant by profit?
5. List 4 examples of decimal.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys. Slower learners receive targeted support during the group work. Learners may first discuss in their mother tongue.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain percent clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
