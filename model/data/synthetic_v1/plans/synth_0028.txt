SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Fractions
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 43

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- explain a curriculum indicator for Fractions using using the chart provided evidence
- calculate the key idea of numerator in one lesson
- demonstrate denominator with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for numerator, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
a wall chart showing numerator.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher revises the previous lesson on addition. This is connected to today's topic, Fractions, and in particular to numerator. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Teacher reads the section on numerator from the textbook.
(20 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of denominator in their exercise books.
(10 minutes) Teacher explains simplify to the class.
(20 minutes) Teacher demonstrates equivalent fractions using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by numerator?
2. Describe how denominator occurs.
3. Explain the role of simplify in your own words.
4. Calculate the answer to the exercise on equivalent fractions on the board.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Provision is made for a learner with a hearing impairment.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain numerator.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners state the key points to the class.
