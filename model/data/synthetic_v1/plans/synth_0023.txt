SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Percentages
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 37

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- appreciate fraction

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for fraction.

RESOURCES:
TLMs will be provided.

INTRODUCTION:
The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Percentages involves fraction. Learners should note that fraction is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves discount. Learners should note that discount is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves percent. Learners should note that percent is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves decimal. Learners should note that decimal is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about fraction.
(10 minutes) Teacher writes notes about discount on the board for learners to copy.
(10 minutes) In groups, learners investigate percent and record their observations.
(10 minutes) In groups, learners investigate decimal and record their observations.

EVALUATION:
1. Calculate the answer to the exercise on fraction on the board.
2. Calculate the answer to the exercise on discount on the board.

DIFFERENTIATION:
No reference to differing learner needs.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain fraction clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
