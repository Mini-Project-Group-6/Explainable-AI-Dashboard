SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Nouns and Pronouns
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 52

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- identify a curriculum indicator for Nouns and Pronouns using with the aid of real objects evidence
- compare the key idea of noun in one lesson
- classify pronoun with a clear example

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for noun.

RESOURCES:
real objects collected from the school compound.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Learners recall what they already know about common noun. This is connected to today's topic, Nouns and Pronouns, and in particular to noun. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Nouns and Pronouns involves noun. Learners should note that noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves pronoun. Learners should note that pronoun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves sentence. Learners should note that sentence is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves subject. Learners should note that subject is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(10 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on noun in pairs.
(15 minutes) Teacher explains pronoun to the class.
(15 minutes) Teacher writes notes about sentence on the board for learners to copy.
(20 minutes) In groups, learners investigate subject and record their observations.
(15 minutes) Learners role-play the process of proper noun in front of the class.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by noun?
2. List 4 examples of pronoun.
3. Explain the role of sentence in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
A support task with scaffolded steps is prepared for learners who struggle. Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain noun clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners state the key points to the class. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
