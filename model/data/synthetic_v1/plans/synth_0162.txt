SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: The Water Cycle
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 35

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- demonstrate a curriculum indicator for The Water Cycle using correctly evidence
- classify the key idea of rainfall in one lesson
- classify condensation with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for rainfall.

RESOURCES:
flashcards for rainfall.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher reviews learners' previous knowledge of rivers. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
The Water Cycle involves rainfall. Learners should note that rainfall is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves condensation. Learners should note that condensation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves clouds. Learners should note that clouds is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves evaporation. Learners should note that evaporation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves precipitation. Learners should note that precipitation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves rivers. Learners should note that rivers is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on rainfall in pairs.
(15 minutes) Teacher demonstrates condensation using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(15 minutes) Learners role-play the process of clouds in front of the class.
(10 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about evaporation.
(10 minutes) In groups, learners investigate precipitation and record their observations.
(10 minutes) Teacher reads the section on rivers from the textbook.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by rainfall?
2. List 4 examples of condensation.

DIFFERENTIATION:
An extension task is ready for learners who finish early. Slower learners receive targeted support during the group work. Learners with special educational needs are seated in front and given adapted materials.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain rainfall.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
