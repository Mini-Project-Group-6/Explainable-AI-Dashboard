SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Percentages
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 50

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:

counters for the group task; a short video clip on fraction.

The objective is shared with learners at the start.

(10 minutes) Learners role-play the process of fraction in front of the class.
(20 minutes) Teacher reads the section on conversion from the textbook.

EVALUATION:
1. Describe how percent occurs.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Learners with special educational needs are seated in front and given adapted materials.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain percent clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
