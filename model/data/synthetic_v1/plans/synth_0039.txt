SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Area of Plane Shapes
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 49

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- solve the main idea of triangle
- solve length

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for triangle.

RESOURCES:
None listed.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher revises the previous lesson on breadth. This is connected to today's topic, Area of Plane Shapes, and in particular to triangle. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Area of Plane Shapes involves triangle. Learners should note that triangle is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves length. Learners should note that length is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves square. Learners should note that square is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves rectangle. Learners should note that rectangle is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(10 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on triangle in pairs.
(10 minutes) Teacher demonstrates length using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(15 minutes) Teacher writes notes about square on the board for learners to copy.
(20 minutes) In groups, learners investigate rectangle and record their observations.

EVALUATION:
1. Describe how triangle occurs.
2. What is meant by length?
3. Calculate the answer to the exercise on square on the board.
4. Explain the role of rectangle in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Key terms are explained in the local language where needed. A support task with scaffolded steps is prepared for learners who struggle. Learners are grouped by ability for the practice task.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain triangle.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
