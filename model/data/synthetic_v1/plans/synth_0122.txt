SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Area of Plane Shapes
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 33

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- be aware of formula

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for formula.

RESOURCES:
a labelled diagram of formula; a phone image of triangle.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Learners recall what they already know about rectangle. This is connected to today's topic, Area of Plane Shapes, and in particular to formula. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Area of Plane Shapes involves formula. Learners should note that formula is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves triangle. Learners should note that triangle is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves square. Learners should note that square is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves length. Learners should note that length is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(10 minutes) Learners discuss formula in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(10 minutes) Learners role-play the process of triangle in front of the class.
(15 minutes) Teacher writes notes about square on the board for learners to copy.
(10 minutes) Teacher explains length to the class.

EVALUATION:
1. List 4 examples of formula.
2. What is meant by triangle?
3. What is meant by square?
4. Explain the role of length in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Learners may first discuss in their mother tongue. Slower learners receive targeted support during the group work.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain formula clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt.
