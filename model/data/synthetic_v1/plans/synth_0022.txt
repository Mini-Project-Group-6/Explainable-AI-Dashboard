SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Percentages
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 52

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- evaluate the main idea of discount
- identify percent

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for discount.

RESOURCES:
counters for the group task; a phone image of percent.

INTRODUCTION:
This is connected to today's topic, Percentages, and in particular to discount.

CORE POINTS:
Percentages involves discount. Learners should note that discount is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves percent. Learners should note that percent is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves profit. Learners should note that profit is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves decimal. Learners should note that decimal is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on discount in pairs.
(20 minutes) Teacher explains percent to the class.
(20 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on profit in pairs.

EVALUATION:
1. Calculate the answer to the exercise on discount on the board.
2. Describe how percent occurs.
3. Describe how profit occurs.
4. Explain the role of decimal in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Learners may first discuss in their mother tongue. Provision is made for a learner with a hearing impairment.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain discount clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
