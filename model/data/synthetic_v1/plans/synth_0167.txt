SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Fractions
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 42 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 51

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- demonstrate a curriculum indicator for Fractions using without referring to the textbook evidence
- state the key idea of denominator in one lesson
- construct numerator with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for denominator.

RESOURCES:
real objects collected from the school compound; a projector where power is available; a whiteboard marker for key terms.

INTRODUCTION:
This is connected to today's topic, Fractions, and in particular to denominator. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
Teacher reads the section on denominator from the textbook.
In groups, learners investigate numerator and record their observations.
Learners draw and label a chart of simplify in their exercise books.
Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about equivalent fractions.

1. Describe how denominator occurs.
2. Explain the role of numerator in your own words.
3. List 4 examples of simplify.
