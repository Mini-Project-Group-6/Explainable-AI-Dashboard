SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Fractions
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 45

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- identify the main idea of simplify
- solve equivalent fractions

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for simplify, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
a short video clip on simplify; specimens of equivalent fractions.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher revises the previous lesson on addition. This is connected to today's topic, Fractions, and in particular to simplify. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Fractions involves simplify. Learners should note that simplify is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves equivalent fractions. Learners should note that equivalent fractions is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves denominator. Learners should note that denominator is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves numerator. Learners should note that numerator is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(10 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on simplify in pairs.
(20 minutes) In groups, learners investigate equivalent fractions and record their observations.
(20 minutes) Teacher reads the section on denominator from the textbook.

EVALUATION:
1. Explain the role of simplify in your own words.
2. What is meant by equivalent fractions?
3. Calculate the answer to the exercise on denominator on the board.
4. List 3 examples of numerator.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Learners with special educational needs are seated in front and given adapted materials. Questions are directed equally across the class. Groups are mixed ability so learners support one another.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain simplify clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
