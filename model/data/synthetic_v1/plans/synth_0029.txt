SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Area of Plane Shapes
CLASS: JHS 1
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 29

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- evaluate a curriculum indicator for Area of Plane Shapes using correctly evidence
- state the key idea of rectangle in one lesson
- evaluate formula with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for rectangle, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
cardboard cut-outs prepared beforehand; a phone image of formula.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher reviews learners' previous knowledge of square.

CORE POINTS:

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) In groups, learners investigate rectangle and record their observations.
(20 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of formula in their exercise books.
(20 minutes) In groups, learners investigate breadth and record their observations.
(10 minutes) Teacher writes notes about length on the board for learners to copy.

EVALUATION:
1. Describe how rectangle occurs.
2. What is meant by formula?
3. Calculate the answer to the exercise on breadth on the board.
4. What is meant by length?

DIFFERENTIATION:
Groups are mixed ability so learners support one another. Questions are directed equally across the class. A challenge question is provided for the most able.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain rectangle.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners state the key points to the class. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
