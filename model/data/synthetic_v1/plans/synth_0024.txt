SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Nouns and Pronouns
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 43

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- evaluate a curriculum indicator for Nouns and Pronouns using without referring to the textbook evidence
- construct the key idea of common noun in one lesson
- calculate noun with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for common noun, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
counters for the group task; a projector where power is available; flashcards for pronoun.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. This is connected to today's topic, Nouns and Pronouns, and in particular to common noun.

CORE POINTS:
Nouns and Pronouns involves common noun. Learners should note that common noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves noun. Learners should note that noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves pronoun. Learners should note that pronoun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves sentence. Learners should note that sentence is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves proper noun. Learners should note that proper noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves subject. Learners should note that subject is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(10 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about common noun.
(10 minutes) Teacher explains noun to the class.
(10 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on pronoun in pairs.
(15 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about sentence.
(20 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about proper noun.
(10 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on subject in pairs.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by common noun?
2. List 4 examples of noun.
3. What is meant by pronoun?

DIFFERENTIATION:
Learners with special educational needs are seated in front and given adapted materials. A support task with scaffolded steps is prepared for learners who struggle. Groups are mixed ability so learners support one another.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain common noun clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
