SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: The Water Cycle
CLASS: JHS 1
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 33

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- calculate the main idea of clouds
- identify condensation

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for clouds.

RESOURCES:
a projector where power is available; a ruler and measuring tape.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher reviews learners' previous knowledge of precipitation. This is connected to today's topic, The Water Cycle, and in particular to clouds.

CORE POINTS:
The Water Cycle involves clouds. Learners should note that clouds is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves condensation. Learners should note that condensation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves rainfall. Learners should note that rainfall is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves rivers. Learners should note that rivers is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves evaporation. Learners should note that evaporation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves precipitation. Learners should note that precipitation is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) In groups, learners investigate clouds and record their observations.
(10 minutes) Learners role-play the process of condensation in front of the class.
(20 minutes) Teacher demonstrates rainfall using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(10 minutes) Teacher reads the section on rivers from the textbook.

EVALUATION:
1. Calculate the answer to the exercise on clouds on the board.
2. List 2 examples of condensation.
3. Explain the role of rainfall in your own words.
4. Describe how rivers occurs.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Key terms are explained in the local language where needed. A challenge question is provided for the most able. Learners with special educational needs are seated in front and given adapted materials.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain clouds.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
