SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Area of Plane Shapes
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 29

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- demonstrate a curriculum indicator for Area of Plane Shapes using correctly evidence
- list the key idea of formula in one lesson
- state breadth with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for formula, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
the class textbook; real objects collected from the school compound; a whiteboard marker for key terms; a projector where power is available.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher reviews learners' previous knowledge of length. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Teacher demonstrates formula using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(20 minutes) Teacher demonstrates breadth using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(15 minutes) Teacher explains triangle to the class.
(20 minutes) Learners role-play the process of square in front of the class.
(20 minutes) Teacher demonstrates rectangle using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(15 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of length in their exercise books.

EVALUATION:
1. Describe how formula occurs.
2. What is meant by breadth?
3. List 3 examples of triangle.
4. Describe how square occurs.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Learners are grouped by ability for the practice task. Learners may first discuss in their mother tongue. Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain formula clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
