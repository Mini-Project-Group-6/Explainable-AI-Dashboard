SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Fractions
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 52

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- be aware of numerator

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for numerator.

RESOURCES:
counters for the group task.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Learners recall what they already know about addition. This is connected to today's topic, Fractions, and in particular to numerator. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Fractions involves numerator. Learners should note that numerator is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves denominator. Learners should note that denominator is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves simplify. Learners should note that simplify is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves equivalent fractions. Learners should note that equivalent fractions is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Learners role-play the process of numerator in front of the class.
(10 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of denominator in their exercise books.
(15 minutes) Teacher writes notes about simplify on the board for learners to copy.
(10 minutes) Teacher explains equivalent fractions to the class.

EVALUATION:
1. List 2 examples of numerator.
2. Calculate the answer to the exercise on denominator on the board.
3. What is meant by simplify?

DIFFERENTIATION:
Learners with special educational needs are seated in front and given adapted materials. Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain numerator clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
