SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Nouns and Pronouns
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 42 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 27

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- construct the main idea of proper noun
- evaluate common noun

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for proper noun, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
a phone image of proper noun; a simple model of common noun.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class.

CORE POINTS:

Nouns and Pronouns involves common noun. Learners should note that common noun is a key point of today's lesson.

Nouns and Pronouns involves sentence. Learners should note that sentence is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves pronoun. Learners should note that pronoun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves noun. Learners should note that noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves proper noun. Learners should note that proper noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves common noun. Learners should note that common noun is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
Learners practise solving exercises on proper noun in pairs.
Learners practise solving exercises on common noun in pairs.
Teacher demonstrates subject using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
Teacher explains sentence to the class.

EVALUATION:
1. Describe how proper noun occurs.
2. Describe how common noun occurs.
3. List 3 examples of subject.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Slower learners receive targeted support during the group work. Learners are grouped by ability for the practice task. Learners with special educational needs are seated in front and given adapted materials.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain proper noun clearly.

SEQUENCING:
A phase is missing and timings are absent.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt.
