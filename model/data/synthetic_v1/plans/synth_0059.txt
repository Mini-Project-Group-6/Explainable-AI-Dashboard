SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: The Water Cycle
CLASS: JHS 1
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 29

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- calculate the main idea of rivers
- list clouds

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for rivers.

RESOURCES:
a phone image of rivers; a whiteboard marker for key terms.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher reviews learners' previous knowledge of evaporation. This is connected to today's topic, The Water Cycle, and in particular to rivers.

CORE POINTS:
The Water Cycle involves rivers. Learners should note that rivers is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves clouds. Learners should note that clouds is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves rainfall. Learners should note that rainfall is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves condensation. Learners should note that condensation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves precipitation. Learners should note that precipitation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves evaporation. Learners should note that evaporation is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Learners discuss rivers in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(15 minutes) Learners role-play the process of clouds in front of the class.
(20 minutes) Teacher explains rainfall to the class.
(10 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about condensation.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by rivers?
2. Describe how clouds occurs.
3. Calculate the answer to the exercise on rainfall on the board.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys. Key terms are explained in the local language where needed.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain rivers clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt.
