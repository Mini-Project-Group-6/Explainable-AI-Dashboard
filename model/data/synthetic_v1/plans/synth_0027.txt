SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Area of Plane Shapes
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 34

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- learn about square

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for square.

RESOURCES:
a whiteboard marker for key terms; flashcards for formula.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. This is connected to today's topic, Area of Plane Shapes, and in particular to square.

CORE POINTS:
Area of Plane Shapes involves square. Learners should note that square is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves formula. Learners should note that formula is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves breadth. Learners should note that breadth is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves length. Learners should note that length is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves triangle. Learners should note that triangle is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves rectangle. Learners should note that rectangle is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of square in their exercise books.
(10 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on formula in pairs.
(10 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about breadth.
(15 minutes) Learners discuss length in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(20 minutes) Teacher reads the section on triangle from the textbook.

EVALUATION:
1. Explain the role of square in your own words.
2. Explain the role of formula in your own words.
3. List 2 examples of breadth.
4. Calculate the answer to the exercise on length on the board.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Slower learners receive targeted support during the group work.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain square.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners state the key points to the class. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
