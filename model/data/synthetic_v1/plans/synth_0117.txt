SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Nouns and Pronouns
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 55

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- explain the main idea of common noun
- solve sentence

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for common noun, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
a labelled diagram of common noun; specimens of sentence.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. This is connected to today's topic, Nouns and Pronouns, and in particular to common noun. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Nouns and Pronouns involves common noun. Learners should note that common noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves sentence. Learners should note that sentence is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves pronoun. Learners should note that pronoun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves subject. Learners should note that subject is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves noun. Learners should note that noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves proper noun. Learners should note that proper noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves common noun. Learners should note that common noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves sentence. Learners should note that sentence is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Learners discuss common noun in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(20 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on sentence in pairs.
(15 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of pronoun in their exercise books.
(10 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on subject in pairs.
(20 minutes) Teacher explains noun to the class.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by common noun?
2. Explain the role of sentence in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys. An extension task is ready for learners who finish early. Provision is made for a learner with a hearing impairment.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain common noun clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
