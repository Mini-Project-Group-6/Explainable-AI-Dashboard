SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Fractions
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 47

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- compare the main idea of equivalent fractions
- calculate addition

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for equivalent fractions.

RESOURCES:
chalkboard and chalk; a ruler and measuring tape.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher revises the previous lesson on simplify. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Fractions involves equivalent fractions. Learners should note that equivalent fractions is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves addition. Learners should note that addition is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves numerator. Learners should note that numerator is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves denominator. Learners should note that denominator is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(10 minutes) Teacher explains equivalent fractions to the class.
(10 minutes) Teacher writes notes about addition on the board for learners to copy.
(10 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on numerator in pairs.
(15 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of denominator in their exercise books.

EVALUATION:
1. Explain the role of equivalent fractions in your own words.
2. Write five sentences about your last holiday.

DIFFERENTIATION:
No reference to differing learner needs.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain equivalent fractions clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners state the key points to the class.
