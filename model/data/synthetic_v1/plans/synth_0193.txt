SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Nouns and Pronouns
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 51

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- identify a curriculum indicator for Nouns and Pronouns using using the chart provided evidence
- calculate the key idea of common noun in one lesson
- explain proper noun with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for common noun, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
counters for the group task; a simple model of proper noun.

INTRODUCTION:
This is connected to today's topic, Nouns and Pronouns, and in particular to common noun. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Nouns and Pronouns involves common noun. Learners should note that common noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves proper noun. Learners should note that proper noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves sentence. Learners should note that sentence is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves pronoun. Learners should note that pronoun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves subject. Learners should note that subject is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves noun. Learners should note that noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves common noun. Learners should note that common noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves proper noun. Learners should note that proper noun is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Learners discuss common noun in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(15 minutes) In groups, learners investigate proper noun and record their observations.
(10 minutes) Teacher writes notes about sentence on the board for learners to copy.
(15 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about pronoun.
(10 minutes) Learners role-play the process of subject in front of the class.
(15 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on noun in pairs.

EVALUATION:
1. Describe how common noun occurs.
2. Calculate the answer to the exercise on proper noun on the board.
3. Calculate the answer to the exercise on sentence on the board.

DIFFERENTIATION:
An extension task is ready for learners who finish early. A support task with scaffolded steps is prepared for learners who struggle. Learners may first discuss in their mother tongue. Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain common noun.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt.
