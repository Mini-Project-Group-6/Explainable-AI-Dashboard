SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Nouns and Pronouns
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 52

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- solve the main idea of common noun
- classify proper noun

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for common noun, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
a whiteboard marker for key terms; a simple model of proper noun.

Learners recall what they already know about noun.
