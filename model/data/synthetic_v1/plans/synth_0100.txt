SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Percentages
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 42 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 40

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- construct a curriculum indicator for Percentages using at least three evidence
- list the key idea of discount in one lesson
- explain percent with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for discount.

RESOURCES:
specimens of discount.

INTRODUCTION:
Learners recall what they already know about profit. This is connected to today's topic, Percentages, and in particular to discount. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Percentages involves discount. Learners should note that discount is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves percent. Learners should note that percent is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves decimal. Learners should note that decimal is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves fraction. Learners should note that fraction is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves conversion. Learners should note that conversion is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves profit. Learners should note that profit is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
Teacher writes notes about discount on the board for learners to copy.
Teacher demonstrates percent using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
In groups, learners investigate decimal and record their observations.
Teacher demonstrates fraction using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about conversion.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by discount?
2. Explain the role of percent in your own words.
3. List 3 examples of decimal.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Slower learners receive targeted support during the group work. An extension task is ready for learners who finish early. Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain discount clearly.

SEQUENCING:
A phase is missing and timings are absent.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
