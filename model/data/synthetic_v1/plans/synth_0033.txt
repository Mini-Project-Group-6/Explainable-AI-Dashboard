SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: Simple Machines
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 39

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- list a curriculum indicator for Simple Machines using with the aid of real objects evidence
- classify the key idea of lever in one lesson
- explain load with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for lever, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
a phone image of lever; a ruler and measuring tape; real objects collected from the school compound.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher revises the previous lesson on effort. This is connected to today's topic, Simple Machines, and in particular to lever. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Simple Machines involves lever. Learners should note that lever is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves load. Learners should note that load is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves inclined plane. Learners should note that inclined plane is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves fulcrum. Learners should note that fulcrum is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves pulley. Learners should note that pulley is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves effort. Learners should note that effort is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves lever. Learners should note that lever is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves load. Learners should note that load is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(10 minutes) Teacher reads the section on lever from the textbook.
(20 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about load.
(20 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about inclined plane.
(20 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of fulcrum in their exercise books.
(20 minutes) Teacher demonstrates pulley using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.

EVALUATION:
1. Calculate the answer to the exercise on lever on the board.
2. Explain the role of load in your own words.
3. List 4 examples of inclined plane.
4. Explain the role of fulcrum in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Groups are mixed ability so learners support one another. Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys. A support task with scaffolded steps is prepared for learners who struggle.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain lever.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners state the key points to the class. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
