SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Percentages
CLASS: JHS 1
DURATION: 42 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 48

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- understand discount

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for discount.

RESOURCES:
flashcards for discount; a ruler and measuring tape.

INTRODUCTION:
The objective is shared with learners at the start.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
Learners draw and label a chart of discount in their exercise books.
In groups, learners investigate decimal and record their observations.
Learners practise solving exercises on fraction in pairs.
Teacher reads the section on profit from the textbook.
Teacher writes notes about conversion on the board for learners to copy.

EVALUATION:
1. List 3 examples of discount.
2. List 4 examples of decimal.
3. Calculate the answer to the exercise on fraction on the board.
4. Calculate the answer to the exercise on profit on the board.
