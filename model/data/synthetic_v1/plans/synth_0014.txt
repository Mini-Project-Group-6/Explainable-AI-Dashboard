SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: Simple Machines
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 25

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- know load

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for load.

RESOURCES:
specimens of load; worksheets on pulley.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher reviews learners' previous knowledge of effort.

CORE POINTS:
Simple Machines involves load. Learners should note that load is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves pulley. Learners should note that pulley is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves fulcrum. Learners should note that fulcrum is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves lever. Learners should note that lever is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves inclined plane. Learners should note that inclined plane is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves effort. Learners should note that effort is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Teacher writes notes about load on the board for learners to copy.
(20 minutes) Teacher explains pulley to the class.
(20 minutes) Teacher demonstrates fulcrum using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(10 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of lever in their exercise books.
(10 minutes) Learners discuss inclined plane in small groups and present their findings to the class.

EVALUATION:
1. Explain the role of load in your own words.
2. Calculate the answer to the exercise on pulley on the board.
3. Calculate the answer to the exercise on fulcrum on the board.
4. What is meant by lever?

DIFFERENTIATION:
An extension task is ready for learners who finish early.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain load clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson.
