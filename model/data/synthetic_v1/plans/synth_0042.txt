SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: Photosynthesis
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 120 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 42

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- classify the main idea of oxygen
- compare sunlight

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for oxygen, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
real objects collected from the school compound; a whiteboard marker for key terms; a labelled diagram of carbon dioxide.

INTRODUCTION:
This is connected to today's topic, Photosynthesis, and in particular to oxygen. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Photosynthesis involves oxygen. Learners should note that oxygen is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves sunlight. Learners should note that sunlight is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves carbon dioxide. Learners should note that carbon dioxide is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves plants. Learners should note that plants is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves chlorophyll. Learners should note that chlorophyll is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves glucose. Learners should note that glucose is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves leaf. Learners should note that leaf is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves oxygen. Learners should note that oxygen is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
Learners role-play the process of oxygen in front of the class.
Teacher explains sunlight to the class.
Learners discuss carbon dioxide in small groups and present their findings to the class.
Learners draw and label a chart of plants in their exercise books.

EVALUATION:
1. Explain the role of oxygen in your own words.
2. Explain the role of sunlight in your own words.
3. List 3 examples of carbon dioxide.
4. Explain the role of plants in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
An extension task is ready for learners who finish early. Slower learners receive targeted support during the group work. Learners with special educational needs are seated in front and given adapted materials.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain oxygen clearly.

SEQUENCING:
A phase is missing and timings are absent.

CLOSURE:
Learners state the key points to the class.
