SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: Photosynthesis
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 36

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- calculate the main idea of sunlight
- classify oxygen

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for sunlight.

RESOURCES:
a labelled diagram of sunlight; a short video clip on oxygen.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher revises the previous lesson on chlorophyll. This is connected to today's topic, Photosynthesis, and in particular to sunlight. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Photosynthesis involves sunlight. Learners should note that sunlight is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves oxygen. Learners should note that oxygen is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves glucose. Learners should note that glucose is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves leaf. Learners should note that leaf is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves plants. Learners should note that plants is a key point of today's lesson.
Photosynthesis involves carbon dioxide. Learners should note that carbon dioxide is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Learners discuss sunlight in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(20 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of oxygen in their exercise books.
(15 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of glucose in their exercise books.
(10 minutes) Teacher explains leaf to the class.

EVALUATION:
1. Describe how sunlight occurs.
2. Calculate the answer to the exercise on oxygen on the board.
3. Explain the role of glucose in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Groups are mixed ability so learners support one another. A challenge question is provided for the most able.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain sunlight.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners state the key points to the class. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
