SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Nouns and Pronouns
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 48

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- classify a curriculum indicator for Nouns and Pronouns using using the chart provided evidence
- compare the key idea of proper noun in one lesson
- state common noun with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for proper noun, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
chalkboard and chalk; a ruler and measuring tape.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher revises the previous lesson on subject. This is connected to today's topic, Nouns and Pronouns, and in particular to proper noun. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Nouns and Pronouns involves proper noun. Learners should note that proper noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves common noun. Learners should note that common noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves pronoun. Learners should note that pronoun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves noun. Learners should note that noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves sentence. Learners should note that sentence is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves subject. Learners should note that subject is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves proper noun. Learners should note that proper noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves common noun. Learners should note that common noun is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(10 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about proper noun.
(20 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on common noun in pairs.
(10 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of pronoun in their exercise books.
(15 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of noun in their exercise books.
(20 minutes) In groups, learners investigate sentence and record their observations.
(20 minutes) Teacher reads the section on subject from the textbook.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by proper noun?
2. Calculate the answer to the exercise on common noun on the board.
3. Calculate the answer to the exercise on pronoun on the board.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Participation is shared fairly between girls and boys. Learners may first discuss in their mother tongue. Learners with special educational needs are seated in front and given adapted materials. A support task with scaffolded steps is prepared for learners who struggle.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain proper noun.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
