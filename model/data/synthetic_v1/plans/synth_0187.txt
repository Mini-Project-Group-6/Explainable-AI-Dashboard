SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: Simple Machines
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 42

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- identify a curriculum indicator for Simple Machines using without referring to the textbook evidence
- calculate the key idea of effort in one lesson
- state lever with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for effort, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
real objects collected from the school compound; cardboard cut-outs prepared beforehand; a wall chart showing pulley; a whiteboard marker for key terms.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher revises the previous lesson on load. This is connected to today's topic, Simple Machines, and in particular to effort. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Simple Machines involves effort. Learners should note that effort is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves lever. Learners should note that lever is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves pulley. Learners should note that pulley is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves inclined plane. Learners should note that inclined plane is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves fulcrum. Learners should note that fulcrum is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves load. Learners should note that load is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves effort. Learners should note that effort is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves lever. Learners should note that lever is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Learners role-play the process of effort in front of the class.
(10 minutes) Learners discuss lever in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(15 minutes) In groups, learners investigate pulley and record their observations.
(20 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on inclined plane in pairs.
(15 minutes) Teacher explains fulcrum to the class.

EVALUATION:
1. Describe how effort occurs.
2. What is meant by lever?
3. Describe how pulley occurs.
4. What is meant by inclined plane?

DIFFERENTIATION:
Provision is made for a learner with a hearing impairment. Learners may first discuss in their mother tongue.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain effort.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
