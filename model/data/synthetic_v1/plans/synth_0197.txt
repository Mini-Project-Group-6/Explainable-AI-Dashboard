SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Area of Plane Shapes
CLASS: JHS 1
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 29

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- know square

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for square.

RESOURCES:
a projector where power is available.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Learners recall what they already know about triangle. This is connected to today's topic, Area of Plane Shapes, and in particular to square. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Area of Plane Shapes involves square. Learners should note that square is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves formula. Learners should note that formula is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves breadth. Learners should note that breadth is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves length. Learners should note that length is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(15 minutes) Teacher reads the section on square from the textbook.
(10 minutes) Teacher demonstrates formula using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(10 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on breadth in pairs.

EVALUATION:
1. Describe how square occurs.

DIFFERENTIATION:
An extension task is ready for learners who finish early. Learners with special educational needs are seated in front and given adapted materials. Groups are mixed ability so learners support one another.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain square clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
