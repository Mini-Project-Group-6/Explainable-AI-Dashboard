SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Area of Plane Shapes
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 36 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 52

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- compare the main idea of triangle
- state square

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for triangle, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
specimens of triangle; worksheets on square; a projector where power is available.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher revises the previous lesson on rectangle. This is connected to today's topic, Area of Plane Shapes, and in particular to triangle. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Area of Plane Shapes involves triangle. Learners should note that triangle is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
Learners draw and label a chart of triangle in their exercise books.
Learners discuss square in small groups and present their findings to the class.
Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about length.
Learners discuss formula in small groups and present their findings to the class.
Teacher reads the section on breadth from the textbook.

EVALUATION:
1. List 4 examples of triangle.
2. What is meant by square?
3. Describe how length occurs.
4. Describe how formula occurs.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Provision is made for a learner with a hearing impairment. Learners may first discuss in their mother tongue.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain triangle.

SEQUENCING:
A phase is missing and timings are absent.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson.
