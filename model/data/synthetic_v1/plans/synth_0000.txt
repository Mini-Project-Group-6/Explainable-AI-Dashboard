SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: The Water Cycle
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 55

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- evaluate the main idea of condensation
- identify clouds

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for condensation.

RESOURCES:
a ruler and measuring tape; chalkboard and chalk; specimens of precipitation; the class textbook.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher reviews learners' previous knowledge of rainfall. This is connected to today's topic, The Water Cycle, and in particular to condensation. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
The Water Cycle involves condensation. Learners should note that condensation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves clouds. Learners should note that clouds is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves precipitation. Learners should note that precipitation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves evaporation. Learners should note that evaporation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves rivers. Learners should note that rivers is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves rainfall. Learners should note that rainfall is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Teacher demonstrates condensation using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(10 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about clouds.
(20 minutes) Teacher explains precipitation to the class.
(10 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about evaporation.
(15 minutes) Learners role-play the process of rivers in front of the class.

EVALUATION:
1. Calculate the answer to the exercise on condensation on the board.
2. Calculate the answer to the exercise on clouds on the board.
3. Explain the role of precipitation in your own words.
4. Calculate the answer to the exercise on evaporation on the board.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Key terms are explained in the local language where needed.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain condensation.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
