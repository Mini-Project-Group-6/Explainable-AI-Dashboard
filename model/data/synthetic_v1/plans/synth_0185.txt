SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Percentages
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 29

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- describe the main idea of discount
- demonstrate fraction

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for discount.

RESOURCES:
worksheets on discount; specimens of fraction; a short video clip on profit.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher revises the previous lesson on conversion. This is connected to today's topic, Percentages, and in particular to discount. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Percentages involves discount. Learners should note that discount is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves fraction. Learners should note that fraction is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves profit. Learners should note that profit is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves decimal. Learners should note that decimal is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves percent. Learners should note that percent is a key point of today's lesson.
Percentages involves conversion. Learners should note that conversion is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) In groups, learners investigate discount and record their observations.
(10 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on fraction in pairs.
(15 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of profit in their exercise books.
(10 minutes) Learners discuss decimal in small groups and present their findings to the class.
(15 minutes) Teacher reads the section on percent from the textbook.
(15 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about conversion.

EVALUATION:
1. List 2 examples of discount.
2. Explain the role of fraction in your own words.
3. List 3 examples of profit.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Questions are directed equally across the class. Learners with special educational needs are seated in front and given adapted materials. A support task with scaffolded steps is prepared for learners who struggle.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain discount.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners state the key points to the class. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
