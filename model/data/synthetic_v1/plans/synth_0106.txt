SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Nouns and Pronouns
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 28

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- learn about noun

PCK:
Content is accurate and appropriate to grade level with worked examples for noun.

RESOURCES:
a phone image of noun.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher revises the previous lesson on common noun. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Nouns and Pronouns involves noun. Learners should note that noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves pronoun. Learners should note that pronoun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves sentence. Learners should note that sentence is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves proper noun. Learners should note that proper noun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves subject. Learners should note that subject is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves common noun. Learners should note that common noun is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(10 minutes) Learners role-play the process of noun in front of the class.
(20 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on pronoun in pairs.
(20 minutes) Teacher reads the section on sentence from the textbook.
(15 minutes) Learners role-play the process of proper noun in front of the class.

EVALUATION:
1. Describe how noun occurs.
2. List 2 examples of pronoun.
3. Write five sentences about your last holiday.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Learners are grouped by ability for the practice task. A support task with scaffolded steps is prepared for learners who struggle.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain noun clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
Lesson ends.
