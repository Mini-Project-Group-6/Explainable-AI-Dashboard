SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Area of Plane Shapes
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 47

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- calculate a curriculum indicator for Area of Plane Shapes using at least two evidence
- calculate the key idea of rectangle in one lesson
- identify formula with a clear example

PCK:
Content is accurate throughout, anticipates common misconceptions for rectangle, and includes pitched worked examples.

RESOURCES:
a ruler and measuring tape; a whiteboard marker for key terms.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Learners recall what they already know about square. This is connected to today's topic, Area of Plane Shapes, and in particular to rectangle. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Area of Plane Shapes involves rectangle. Learners should note that rectangle is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves formula. Learners should note that formula is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves breadth. Learners should note that breadth is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves triangle. Learners should note that triangle is a key point of today's lesson.
Area of Plane Shapes involves length. Learners should note that length is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Teacher writes notes about rectangle on the board for learners to copy.
(15 minutes) Learners role-play the process of formula in front of the class.
(10 minutes) Learners role-play the process of breadth in front of the class.
(10 minutes) Teacher demonstrates triangle using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(15 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on length in pairs.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by rectangle?
2. What is meant by formula?
3. Explain the role of breadth in your own words.
4. Calculate the answer to the exercise on triangle on the board.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Slower learners receive targeted support during the group work. Key terms are explained in the local language where needed.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain rectangle.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Learners state the key points to the class. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
