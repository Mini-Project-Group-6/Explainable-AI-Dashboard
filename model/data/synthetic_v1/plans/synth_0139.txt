SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Fractions
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 80 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 31

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- identify a curriculum indicator for Fractions using using the chart provided evidence
- evaluate the key idea of simplify in one lesson
- solve numerator with a clear example

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for simplify.

RESOURCES:
a projector where power is available; counters for the group task.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher reviews learners' previous knowledge of denominator. This is connected to today's topic, Fractions, and in particular to simplify. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Fractions involves simplify. Learners should note that simplify is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves numerator. Learners should note that numerator is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves addition. Learners should note that addition is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves equivalent fractions. Learners should note that equivalent fractions is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) In groups, learners investigate simplify and record their observations.
(20 minutes) Teacher demonstrates numerator using TLMs while learners observe and ask questions.
(20 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about addition.
(10 minutes) Teacher reads the section on equivalent fractions from the textbook.

EVALUATION:
1. Calculate the answer to the exercise on simplify on the board.
2. Calculate the answer to the exercise on numerator on the board.
3. Describe how addition occurs.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Questions are directed equally across the class. An extension task is ready for learners who finish early.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain simplify clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Learners summarise in their own words what they have learnt. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
