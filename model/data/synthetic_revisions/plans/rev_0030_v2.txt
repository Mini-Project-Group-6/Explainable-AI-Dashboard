SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Fractions
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 42 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 47

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- explain the main idea of simplify
- evaluate denominator

PCK:
The plan contains a factual or conceptual error, or content is absent.

RESOURCES:
cardboard cut-outs prepared beforehand.

INTRODUCTION:
Learners recall what they already know about numerator. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Fractions involves simplify. Learners should note that simplify is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves denominator. Learners should note that denominator is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
In groups, learners investigate simplify and record their observations.
Learners practise solving exercises on denominator in pairs.
Learners draw and label a chart of addition in their exercise books.
Teacher writes notes about equivalent fractions on the board for learners to copy.
Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about numerator.

EVALUATION:
1. Describe how simplify occurs.
2. List 4 examples of denominator.
3. What is meant by addition?
4. Describe how equivalent fractions occurs.
5. List 4 examples of numerator.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Learners with special educational needs are seated in front and given adapted materials.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain simplify clearly.
