PCK:

INTRODUCTION:
The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Photosynthesis involves plants. Learners should note that plants is a key point of today's lesson and this is something that the learners should really try to remember because it will come up again later in the term when we treat the next topic which is related to this one in several ways.
Photosynthesis involves glucose. Learners should note that glucose is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
Learners discuss plants in small groups and present their findings to the class.
Learners draw and label a chart of glucose in their exercise books.
In groups, learners investigate oxygen and record their observations.
Teacher writes notes about chlorophyll on the board for learners to copy.

EVALUATION:
1. List 4 examples of plants.
2. Describe how glucose occurs.
