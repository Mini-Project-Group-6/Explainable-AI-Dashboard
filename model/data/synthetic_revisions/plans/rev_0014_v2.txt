SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: The Water Cycle
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 120 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 48

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- appreciate rivers

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for rivers.

RESOURCES:
TLMs will be provided.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Teacher revises the previous lesson on clouds. This is connected to today's topic, The Water Cycle, and in particular to rivers. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
The Water Cycle involves rivers. Learners should note that rivers is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves condensation. Learners should note that condensation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves evaporation. Learners should note that evaporation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves precipitation. Learners should note that precipitation is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
Teacher reads the section on rivers from the textbook.
Learners discuss condensation in small groups and present their findings to the class.
In groups, learners investigate evaporation and record their observations.
Learners role-play the process of precipitation in front of the class.
In groups, learners investigate rainfall and record their observations.

EVALUATION:
1. Write five sentences about your last holiday.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Groups are mixed ability so learners support one another.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain rivers clearly.

SEQUENCING:
A phase is missing and timings are absent.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
