SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: The Water Cycle
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 48 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 37

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- evaluate the main idea of rainfall
- identify evaporation

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for rainfall.

RESOURCES:
a short video clip on rainfall.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class.

CORE POINTS:
The Water Cycle involves rainfall. Learners should note that rainfall is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves evaporation. Learners should note that evaporation is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves rivers. Learners should note that rivers is a key point of today's lesson.
The Water Cycle involves condensation. Learners should note that condensation is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
Teacher reads the section on rainfall from the textbook.
Teacher writes notes about evaporation on the board for learners to copy.
Learners practise solving exercises on rivers in pairs.
Learners practise solving exercises on condensation in pairs.

EVALUATION:
1. Explain the role of rainfall in your own words.
2. Describe how evaporation occurs.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Groups are mixed ability so learners support one another.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain rainfall clearly.

SEQUENCING:
No discernible phase structure.

CLOSURE:
Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
