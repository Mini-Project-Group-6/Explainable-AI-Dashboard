SUBJECT: Mathematics
TOPIC: Fractions
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 90 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 46

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- state the main idea of denominator
- compare equivalent fractions

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for denominator.

RESOURCES:
real objects collected from the school compound; worksheets on equivalent fractions.

INTRODUCTION:
Learners recall what they already know about numerator. This is connected to today's topic, Fractions, and in particular to denominator. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Fractions involves denominator. Learners should note that denominator is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves equivalent fractions. Learners should note that equivalent fractions is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves simplify. Learners should note that simplify is a key point of today's lesson.
Fractions involves addition. Learners should note that addition is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
In groups, learners investigate denominator and record their observations.
Learners discuss equivalent fractions in small groups and present their findings to the class.
Teacher writes notes about simplify on the board for learners to copy.
Learners role-play the process of addition in front of the class.

EVALUATION:
1. What is meant by denominator?
2. List 2 examples of equivalent fractions.
3. What is meant by simplify?
4. Explain the role of addition in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Groups are mixed ability so learners support one another. A support task with scaffolded steps is prepared for learners who struggle.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain denominator clearly.

SEQUENCING:
A phase is missing and timings are absent.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Teacher asks two questions to confirm attainment before dismissal.
