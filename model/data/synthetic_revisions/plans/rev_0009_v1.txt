SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: Simple Machines
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 48

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- learn about fulcrum

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for fulcrum.

RESOURCES:
a simple model of fulcrum; a whiteboard marker for key terms; worksheets on pulley.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. This is connected to today's topic, Simple Machines, and in particular to fulcrum.

CORE POINTS:
Simple Machines involves fulcrum. Learners should note that fulcrum is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves inclined plane. Learners should note that inclined plane is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves pulley. Learners should note that pulley is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves load. Learners should note that load is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(10 minutes) Learners role-play the process of fulcrum in front of the class.
(15 minutes) Teacher writes notes about inclined plane on the board for learners to copy.
(15 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on pulley in pairs.
(15 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about load.

EVALUATION:
1. List 3 examples of fulcrum.
2. Copy the notes from the board neatly into your exercise book.

DIFFERENTIATION:
Provision is made for a learner with a hearing impairment.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain fulcrum.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.

CLOSURE:
Teacher summarises the key points of the lesson.
