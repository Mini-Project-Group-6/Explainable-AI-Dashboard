SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: Photosynthesis
CLASS: JHS 2
DURATION: 90 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 34

OBJECTIVES:
Teacher explains the lesson topic.

PCK:
The plan contains a factual or conceptual error, or content is absent.

RESOURCES:
counters for the group task.

INTRODUCTION:
The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Photosynthesis involves plants. Learners should note that plants is a key point of today's lesson and this is something that the learners should really try to remember because it will come up again later in the term when we treat the next topic which is related to this one in several ways.
Photosynthesis involves glucose. Learners should note that glucose is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
Learners draw and label a chart of plants in their exercise books.
Teacher reads the section on glucose from the textbook.
In groups, learners investigate oxygen and record their observations.
Learners discuss chlorophyll in small groups and present their findings to the class.
Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about sunlight.

EVALUATION:
1. List 4 examples of plants.
2. Describe how glucose occurs.
3. Explain the role of oxygen in your own words.

DIFFERENTIATION:
A support task with scaffolded steps is prepared for learners who struggle. Provision is made for a learner with a hearing impairment.
