SUBJECT: English Language
TOPIC: Nouns and Pronouns
CLASS: JHS 3
DURATION: 70 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 30

OBJECTIVES:
Teacher explains the lesson topic.

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for sentence.

RESOURCES:
a short video clip on sentence; flashcards for subject.

INTRODUCTION:
Teacher opens with a short hook to settle the class. Learners recall what they already know about noun. This is connected to today's topic, Nouns and Pronouns, and in particular to sentence. The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Nouns and Pronouns involves sentence. Learners should note that sentence is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves subject. Learners should note that subject is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves pronoun. Learners should note that pronoun is a key point of today's lesson.
Nouns and Pronouns involves common noun. Learners should note that common noun is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(10 minutes) Teacher reads the section on sentence from the textbook.
(15 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of subject in their exercise books.
(15 minutes) Teacher explains pronoun to the class.

EVALUATION:
Exercise to be given later.

DIFFERENTIATION:
No reference to differing learner needs.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses an analogy, diagram, and demonstration from the Ghanaian classroom context to explain sentence.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary are present and ordered. Timings are allocated.
