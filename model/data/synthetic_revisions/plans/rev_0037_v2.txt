SUBJECT: Integrated Science
TOPIC: Simple Machines
CLASS: JHS 1
DURATION: 60 minutes
CLASS SIZE: 46

OBJECTIVES:
By the end of the lesson, the learner will be able to:
- learn about pulley

PCK:
Content is broadly correct but thin and generic for pulley.

RESOURCES:
a wall chart showing pulley; a simple model of fulcrum.

INTRODUCTION:
The objective is shared with learners at the start.

CORE POINTS:
Simple Machines involves pulley. Learners should note that pulley is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves fulcrum. Learners should note that fulcrum is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves lever. Learners should note that lever is a key point of today's lesson.
Simple Machines involves load. Learners should note that load is a key point of today's lesson.

TEACHER-LEARNER ACTIVITIES:
(20 minutes) Learners draw and label a chart of pulley in their exercise books.
(20 minutes) Teacher reads the section on fulcrum from the textbook.
(15 minutes) Learners role-play the process of lever in front of the class.
(15 minutes) Learners practise solving exercises on load in pairs.
(10 minutes) Through question and answer, learners brainstorm what they know about effort.

EVALUATION:
1. Copy the notes from the board neatly into your exercise book.
2. Copy the notes from the board neatly into your exercise book.

DIFFERENTIATION:
A support task with scaffolded steps is prepared for learners who struggle.

EXPLANATION STRATEGY:
Teacher uses familiar examples to explain pulley clearly.

SEQUENCING:
Starter, main, and plenary all present with coherent progression; each activity builds on the previous; timings are realistic and balanced. Phase timings are shown throughout.

CLOSURE:
The main points are consolidated on the board. Learners state the key points to the class. Closing questions check whether the objective was met.
